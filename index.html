LAX Parking

LAX long-term parking rates range around $7-$17 per day. Garages inside the airport are capped at $40 per day, so an off-airport spot will save you a significant amount. Off-site economy lots start at $9 per day and indoor LAX valet parking starts as low as $12.75. The newest structure, Economy Lot E, is located off 111th Street, between Aviation and La Cienega boulevards. The daily rate at LAX Lot E is $12.

The best parking near LAX for dropping off or picking up passengers is at any of the garages located along World Way. These lots charge $4 for the first hour and then $4 for each additional hour or fraction thereof. There is a Cell Phone Waiting Lot located on W 96th street, where drivers and those waiting for you may arrive up to 2 hours early for free. Drivers are expected to wait in their vehicles and are not allowed to leave their vehicle unattended.

If you drive an oversized vehicle, Sunrise LAX Parking can accommodate you at $17 per day. Most car parking lots are located about a 7 to 15 minute shuttle ride from the terminals. The shuttles typically run every 10-20 minutes to and from the airport, contingent on traffic conditions. Most airport shuttle services operate 24/7 and are free with your reservation.

Reserve your LAX Parking

Always plan ahead and reserve a spot in advance when leaving a vehicle behind, especially during heavy travel weekends and holidays. There are several lots we recommend you reserve and pay for ahead of time so that on the day of your departure, parking and catching your flight will be the easiest part of your day.

Select from any of these parking lots for an easy and convenient parking reservation!

How much is parking at LAX?

Parking at LAX can range anywhere between $12 per day in Economy Lot E to $59.99 per day in the Central Terminal Area (CTA) parking. Off airport parking rates start at $8.95 per day.

How does LAX long-term airport parking work?

Long-term parking at LAX is quick, economical, and hassle free. Off-site airport parking means leaving your car outside of the terminal area at a secure, trustworthy location. Park your car at the location of your choice, hop on a complimentary shuttle, and you are at the airport in no time. Upon your return to LAX, the shuttle will pick you up and drop you off at your car's location. LAX parking reservations can be made here.

What is the cheapest outdoor parking lot at LAX?

The most economical outdoor parking at LAX is at Vpne Parking. Rates start at $8.95 per day. Shuttles to and from the airport are included with every reservation.

What is the cheapest indoor parking lot at LAX?

The most economical indoor parking at LAX is 405 Airport Parking. Rates start at as $10.95 per day! Shuttles to and from the airport are included with every reservation.

Is parking my car outside of the airport safe?

We only partner with the safest, most secure lots and hotels around LAX. We require all of our partner lots to have 24-hour security, surveillance cameras, and either fenced-in or guarded properties.

How much do I save when I book LAX airport parking online?

Online rates are at 30 to 50% lower when parking is booked in advance.

Do LAX airport parking lots offer shuttles?

Yes. LAX offers public shuttle services if you parked in Economy Public Parking Lot E. Shuttles run every 15 minutes to and from the airport. Complimentary private shuttle transportation to and from the airport with direct terminal drop-off is included with every reservation.

Can I park my car inside LAX airport?

Yes, there is parking available inside LAX at central terminal area. Currrently terminal parking is offered at $59.99 per day. Off site parking available at a lower cost outside of LAX.
